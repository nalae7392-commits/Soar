These movies exist in that golden space between charm and chaos – the modern comfort rom-com. They're drenched in early-2000s nostalgia, the kind of films that make you believe in serendipity, big gestures, and the power of reinvention. Their worlds are bright and a little absurd, where love happens in bookstores, offices, and makeover montages – and somehow, it always feels right.
