I once wrote the emotions I embraced, the sentences that struck my head, the inspirations that lifted me. Now stuck, drifting a long tunnel that may, I hope, enlarge the volume of my life. For a life of essence and sharp. Not to waste my time, youth, and passion in unconscious drift, but to know where my words take aim, where my steps are carried forward, where my vision and striving are borne. In the hope that every action, every decision, may enlighten myself.
