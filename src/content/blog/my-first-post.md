I've wanted a place to write things down that isn't a notes app nobody else will ever see, and isn't a polished essay either. This is that place: a journal, not a highlight reel.

Expect short entries about what I'm building, what I'm reading, problems I ran into and how I got unstuck, and the occasional half-formed idea I want to remember having. Some posts will be a few lines. Some will ramble. I'm not going to smooth that out.

No promises on how often I'll post — just whenever there's something worth writing down. First entry, and the rest will follow.
